Departure — Balingasa, Commonwealth
📍 106 Metom St, Commonwealth, Quezon City
Pick-Up Point via NLEX Mindanao Ave
📍 Map

The adventure begins at the stroke of midnight! A late-night departure is the classic road tripper's move — beat the Metro Manila traffic, arrive fresh, and make the most of the day ahead. Enter NLEX via the Mindanao Avenue toll gate for a smooth northbound journey.

💡 Tips for Midnight Departure
  • Fill up the tank the night before — gas stations may be scarce after midnight on some NLEX stretches
  • Designate drivers and plan your rest stop shifts before leaving
  • Prepare playlist, snacks, and pillows for backseat passengers
  • Expect to pay NLEX toll at Mindanao Ave — have EasyTrip or exact change ready
  • Drive time to Manaoag is approximately 4.5–5 hours without traffic

Manaoag Church
📍 Minor Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary, Manaoag, Pangasinan
Pilgrimage Rest Stop ⭐ 4.8 / 5
📍 Map

A beloved tradition for road trippers heading north, the Minor Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary of Manaoag is one of the most revered Marian shrines in the Philippines. Arriving at 5 AM means you'll catch the early morning Mass and experience the church in its most peaceful, spiritually uplifting state — mist still in the air, candles flickering, pilgrims deep in prayer. This is also your perfect pit stop: stretch your legs, freshen up, and enjoy your home-cooked breakfast (baon) in the peaceful church grounds.

💡 Tips for Manaoag
  • The church opens at 4:00 AM — you'll likely arrive right at its most serene hour
  • Paid parking is available around the church area; no parking inside the church compound
  • Dress modestly — cover shoulders and knees inside the basilica
  • Buy candles at the church store for prayers (a lovely tradition)
  • Restrooms are available near the compound — perfect for a full road-trip freshen-up
  • Enjoy your baon breakfast on the church grounds or in the car before heading out

Gapuz Grapes Farm
📍 MacArthur Hwy, Bauang, La Union
Farm Experience Grape Picking ⭐ 4.3 / 5
📍 Map

Welcome to La Union! One of the region's most beloved agri-tourism attractions, Gapuz Grapes Farm is the original family-owned vineyard that helped establish Bauang as grape country. May is right in harvest season — you'll likely get to pick plump grapes straight from the vine! The farm also sells grape wine, fresh grapes, and locally made products perfect as pasalubong.

💡 Tips for Gapuz Farm
  • Call ahead (+63 917 554 0966) to confirm availability — they appreciate notice and will have a guide ready
  • Follow Google Maps exactly — impostors near the road will try to redirect you to other farms. Trust the GPS!
  • May is peak harvest season — expect ₱350/kg for grapes (subject to change)
  • The road leading in is bumpy — not recommended for very lowered cars
  • Pick up grape wine as pasalubong — best enjoyed chilled!
  • Allot ~1.5–2 hours for the full experience
★★★★ 4.3 · 1,146 reviews · Open daily 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM · Tel: +63 917 554 0966

The Mansion La Union
📍 Upper Talogtog, San Juan, La Union
Scenic Viewpoint Coffee Stop ⭐ 4.5 / 5
📍 Map

Perched on a mountaintop overlooking the stunning coastline of San Juan Beach, The Mansion La Union is pure Instagram gold — but even better in person. The panoramic view of the South China Sea below is absolutely breathtaking, especially on a clear May morning. Grab a cortado or iced latte from the café, take your group photos, breathe in the fresh highland air, and soak in what's arguably one of the best views in La Union.

💡 Tips for The Mansion
  • The road up is very steep with 3 sharp inclines — make sure your vehicle is in good condition before heading up
  • There are separate stairs for pedestrians if anyone prefers to walk up
  • Best views on a clear day — May weather is typically sunny and fair, perfect timing!
  • Coffee menu is limited but good — their iced latte is recommended by regulars
  • Coordinate with traffic going up/down the single-lane road before proceeding

Urbiztondo Beach — Surf, Swim & Nightlife
📍 Urbiztondo, San Juan, La Union
Surfing Swimming Nightlife ⭐ 4.4 / 5
📍 Map

This is what La Union is famous for! Urbiztondo in San Juan is the surf capital of Northern Luzon — a sun-soaked strip of golden sand lined with surf schools, beach bars, restaurants, and local shops. With 4.5 glorious hours from 3 PM, you'll have time to catch some waves (or learn!), swim in the afternoon sun, watch the epic sunset from the shoreline, and explore the vibrant nightlife scene as the beach transforms after dark. Park at Park & Surf for secure, convenient car storage.

💡 Tips for Urbiztondo
  • Park at Park & Surf: ₱200 parking until 1 AM, accepts cash and online payment. Keep your ticket stub for re-entry
  • Surfing lessons available on the beach — beginners welcome, instructors are friendly and non-pushy
  • May afternoon waves are great for beginners — not too big, not too small
  • Bring waterproof phone case or dry bag for your phone and valuables at the beach
  • Reef-safe sunscreen is encouraged — the coastal environment will thank you
  • Many great food stalls and restaurants along the strip — try local seafood!
  • The area gets lively at sunset and into the evening — great bar-hopping vibes
  • Wear water shoes — some areas have rocks on the shoreline

Morning Prep & Breakfast at Auntie Ludy's
📍 Camansi, San Fernando City, La Union
Breakfast Morning Prep

Rise and shine! Wake up with the mountain breeze of La Union, enjoy a home-cooked breakfast at Auntie Ludy's, pack your bags, and get ready for a scenic day in Baguio City. The drive via Naguillian Road (an alternate and more scenic route to Baguio) takes approximately 1.5–2 hours.

Scenic Drive — Naguillian Road to Baguio
📍 San Fernando, La Union → Baguio City via Naguillian Rd
Scenic Route ~2 Hours
📍 Map

The Naguillian Road (also called La Union–Baguio Road) is one of the most scenic alternate routes to Baguio — winding up through mountains, past terraced hillsides, and offering jaw-dropping views of the La Union coast below. It's a slightly longer route than Kennon Road but typically more reliable and absolutely gorgeous on a clear morning.

💡 Naguillian Road Tips
  • The road is winding and narrow in some sections — drive carefully, especially if unfamiliar
  • Watch out for local buses and trucks on hairpin turns — yield where needed
  • Fuel up in San Fernando before departing — gas stations become scarce on the mountain stretch
  • Stop at scenic pullouts for photos — the views of the coast are spectacular at altitude!

Tan-aw sa Alang
📍 Ben Palispis Hwy, Tuba, Benguet
Restaurant Mountain Views ⭐ 4.4 / 5
📍 Map

A true hidden gem along the Baguio approach road, Tan-aw sa Alang is a rustic mountain café and restaurant perched with a stunning overlooking view of forested valleys and mini terraces. The name itself means "view from above" in Ilocano — and it delivers. Their brewed Benguet coffee served with balikutsa (native caramel candy) is legendary, and the Tokwa & Bagnet dish is a crowd favorite that's worth ordering twice. When the weather is misty, you'll quite literally be dining in the clouds.

💡 Tips for Tan-aw sa Alang
  • Must order: Brewed Benguet coffee with balikutsa, and the Tokwa & Bagnet (highly rated!)
  • Serves all-day breakfast — bangsilog, longganisa, banana bread are also great picks
  • Pet-friendly with good parking and clean restrooms
  • Pick up pasalubong from their native goods section
  • Plan for 2 hours here — the views invite you to linger
  • Open Sat until 7:00 PM; breakfast/brunch is your sweet spot

L'Atelier Du Grain
📍 37 Military Cutoff Rd, Baguio City
Artisan Bakery Pastries ⭐ 4.6 / 5
📍 Map

Easily one of Baguio's most talked-about bakeries, L'Atelier Du Grain is an artisanal pastry shop that has earned cult status among food lovers. Their buttery, flaky croissants and delicate tart shells are genuinely exceptional — the strawberry cheesecake tart in particular is a must-try. The shop is small and gets crowded, but the quality of every bite makes it worth the squeeze. A short and sweet stop that makes a big impression.

💡 Tips for L'Atelier Du Grain
  • Must-try: Croissant, Strawberry Cheesecake Tart, seasonal pastries
  • Minimum order of ₱500 — but easily met with their irresistible selection
  • Accepts credit cards (convenient!)
  • Parking is tight in the area — Military Cutoff Rd can get busy; arrive with patience
  • Get pastries to-go and enjoy them at nearby Burnham Park or Session Road area
  • A non-negotiable Baguio stop according to reviewers — don't skip this one!

Meet Relatives in Baguio
📍 Baguio City (location flexible)
Family Time Flexible Schedule

Quality time with family is priceless! This 2-hour window is set aside to visit and reconnect with relatives in Baguio — one of the highlights of any trip to the City of Pines. The timing is flexible depending on the relatives' availability, so coordinate with them ahead of time. If there's extra time, Baguio's iconic spots are just minutes away.

Cafe Khawa — Early Dinner & Sunset
📍 Loakan, Baguio City, Benguet
Early Dinner Sunset Views Mountain Café
📍 Map

The perfect sendoff to your La Union–Baguio adventure! Cafe Khawa in Loakan is a tucked-away mountain escape with scenic views, cool pine-fresh air, and a welcoming, laid-back atmosphere. It's the ideal spot to enjoy an early dinner as the sun dips toward the horizon before you hit the road home. The ambiance is calming and photogenic — beautiful woodwork, mountain vistas, and the kind of quiet that makes you wish the trip wasn't ending.

💡 Tips for Cafe Khawa
  • Arrive by 4:00 PM to secure good seats with views before the sunset crowd
  • Best seats: outdoor or window-facing for the sunset panorama
  • Bring a jacket — Loakan's elevation makes it cooler than central Baguio
  • Check their food menu ahead of time; some items have limited availability
  • Good parking available — a rare convenience in Baguio!
  • Target departure by 6:00–6:30 PM to make it back to Commonwealth at a reasonable hour
